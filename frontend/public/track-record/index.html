What the % means

Summary by strategy

Cumulative result

Running sum of daily %, per strategy. Flat stretches are days it did not trade.

Every trading day

One bar per day the strategy traded. Losing days are shown as they happened.

By month

Sum of daily % in each calendar month, with the days traded.

How robust is it

Computed from the daily results above. A simulated year redraws a year's worth of the strategy's own days, at random, 10,000 times; a 1-in-20 figure is one that only 5% of those years are worse than.

Deflated Sharpe: the probability that the daily result is better than the luckiest of that many rule variants with no real edge. All of this is in-sample, like the record itself.
